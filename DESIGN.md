# Design plan, revised against the supplied main brief

Light first: #F1F2F6 page, white raised base, #3A5BD9 interactions. Semantic colors: #0E9F6E good, #C2710C caution, #D0342C bad. Text uses 92/60/40% ink. Thin/regular/thick materials use the exact white alpha, blur, saturation, hairline, and shadows in section 6. Dark mode has independent surface tokens. System sans is the offline fallback for Geist. Numbers use tabular lining figures. Motion is reserved for sheets and wizard steps.
